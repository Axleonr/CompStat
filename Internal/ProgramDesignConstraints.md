**P1 — Derivation, not independence.**

The goal list is the sole specification. The Quick Checklist is a *derived view* of it: checklist item *n* of module *M* is the interrogative rendering of Goal *M.n*. It may weaken a performative verb only where a problem set tagged to *M.n* carries that requirement. Any other change to what is required is a change to the goal, not to the checklist.

**Co-location of the two lists is intentional and they are not to be consolidated.** Once the goals and the checklist sit fifteen lines apart on the same page, the pressure to deduplicate is constant. Without this clause a future correction pass will re-derive the merge ruling from first principles.

**P2 — Locality.**

Every vocabulary a student meets inside a module must be resolvable inside that module. This is the standing test for where any future definition goes.

Behind it is a hard constraint: **final artifacts are static PDFs and modules ship separately.** A student working Module 4's file may never have Module 0's. Cross-references to another document are not resolutions under P2.
